Researched 2026-10-06. Official agency/state sources only. Nothing here is copied into app data yet.

verification" in a browser). It was NOT bypassed. Nebraska rules below come from the regulations themselves

Elk (eff. 2026-01-03); 166 NAC 14 = Mountain Sheep; 166 NAC 1 = general rules incl. points. Title 163 is now only
Administration (163 NAC 5 = fees).

## PART 1 — DRAW RULES

### New Mexico (2026-27 license year)

Facts (2026-2027 Hunting Rules & Info booklet, p.~18 "How the Draw Hunt System Works"; 19.31.3 NMAC):
- **Pure random draw, no points.** Each application gets a random sequence number; 1st/2nd/3rd choices are matched
  in that order. "New Mexico does not grant preference to previously unsuccessful applicants." Optional 4th choice
  for deer/elk = any undersubscribed hunt with the same sporting arm as 1st choice.
- **Quotas (state law, §17-3-16 NMSA; booklet):** min **84%** NM residents; **10%** residents + nonresidents applying
  with a NM-registered outfitter; **6%** nonresidents without an outfitter. Applies to all draw hunts (deer, elk,
  pronghorn, bighorn, ibex, oryx, Barbary sheep, javelina). **100% resident**: antlerless elk and hunts held solely
  on WMAs. Population-management hunts are not subject to quota. Booklet warns nonresidents rarely draw hunt codes
  with ≤12 licenses, outfitted applicants ≤6.
- **License required to apply:** must buy a nonrefundable 2026-27 Game-Hunting (or Game-Hunting & Fishing) license
  + HMAV (Habitat Management & Access Validation, 18+) for Barbary sheep, bighorn, deer, elk, ibex, javelina, oryx,
  pronghorn. Full license fee charged up front, refunded if unsuccessful; application fee not refunded.
  Bear/turkey/crane/pheasant draws need no license to apply.
- **Deadline:** big game **March** (March 18, 2026). Bear & turkey: February. Pheasant/crane: August.
  Must have filed prior-year harvest reports by the deadline or all draw applications are rejected.
- **Once-in-a-lifetime:** Rocky Mountain bighorn ram (1 per life) and desert bighorn ram (1 per life — holding one
  doesn't bar the other); ibex IBX-1-101 any-legal hunt; WSMR either-sex oryx hunts (off-range and population-
  management oryx hunts are NOT OIL); Valle Vidal (Unit 55A) mature-bull/either-sex elk and Valle Vidal antlerless
  (residents only). Separate once-in-a-youth versions exist.
- **Leftovers:** 19.31.3.8 NMAC — licenses left after the draw "may be sold online via secondary sale." In practice
  NMDOW sells leftover draw licenses first-come-first-served online in late **June**: residents only for the first
  24 h, then nonresidents; must already hold the current Game-Hunting license (2024: June 26; 2025: June 25).
  Youth encouragement elk licenses also sold FCFS online (resident youth who struck out get first 14 days).

**DRAW RULES (NM):**
> New Mexico uses a purely random draw with no preference or bonus points, so every applicant has the same odds
> each year no matter how many times they have applied. By state law at least 84% of draw licenses go to residents,
> 10% to applicants using a New Mexico-registered outfitter, and 6% to nonresidents without an outfitter; cow elk
> and Wildlife Management Area hunts are resident-only. You must buy a nonrefundable Game-Hunting license (plus the
> habitat validation) before applying for big game, and applications for deer, elk, pronghorn, bighorn, ibex, oryx,
> Barbary sheep and javelina are due in March. Bighorn rams (one Rocky Mountain and one desert per lifetime), the
> ibex any-legal hunt, White Sands either-sex oryx and Valle Vidal elk are once-in-a-lifetime. Leftover licenses go
> on sale online first-come-first-served in late June, residents first for 24 hours.

### Nebraska (2026)

Facts:
- **Elk (general/non-landowner bull): BONUS points, residents only.** 166 NAC 1 §001.01A3c: denied residents get a
  bonus point; each point adds an extra entry (n+1 chances); points lost when drawn. NGPC page (search index) says
  the general bull draw "is moving to a bonus point squared system" — regulation text still describes linear
  entries; confirm before relying on squared odds. 166 NAC 3 §001.02A1: "Residents may apply for a Bonus Point or
  one elk permit." Nonresidents only via landowner elk permits (NR landowner in an elk unit).
- **Landowner bull elk: PREFERENCE points** (most points first); NR landowners earn 0.9 point/yr (166 NAC 1 §001.01A3a).
- **Elk limits:** antlered elk once every 5 years AND only one antlered elk per lifetime (except landowner limited
  or auction/lottery permits) — Neb. Rev. Stat. §37-450(3) (amended LB979, eff. 2026-07-18). Antlerless elk no more
  than once every 5 years (166 NAC 3).
- **Antelope: PREFERENCE points.** Residents apply for draw units (Banner N/S, Box Butte E/W, Cherry, Cheyenne,
  Dismal, Eastern Sandhills, Garden, North Sioux, Prairie MZ, North Sioux MZ); nonresidents only Antelope Archery
  (draw) or leftovers. Highest points drawn first; 2nd-choice applicants with more points beat 1st-choice ones.
  Points lost on drawing a firearm/MZ buck or either-sex permit.
- **Deer: PREFERENCE points for draw units only.** Resident draw units: Platte Any Deer, Frenchman Any Deer.
  Nonresident draw units: Sandhills, Upper Platte, Plains, Calamus West, Pine Ridge, Frenchman, Platte, Buffalo,
  Loup West Any Deer, Republican Mule, plus statewide Archery Deer and Muzzleloader Deer (NR quotas 3,000/1,000 per
  NGPC page). Everything else is a "buy" (OTC, first-come) unit; residents buy statewide archery/muzzleloader OTC.
  Residents beat nonresidents regardless of points except within NR quotas.
- **Bighorn sheep: lottery, NOT points; residents only** (§37-451; 166 NAC 14 §001.01A). One application/year;
  all valid applications "considered equally in a drawing." Plus up to 2 auction permits open to residents and
  nonresidents. Once-in-a-lifetime ram for residents (NGPC lottery page, search index). 2026: 1 lottery + 1 auction.
  Must attend orientation and hunt with a Commission representative.
- **Application periods:** Elk/antelope/deer draw (Period 1): **June** (first Monday in June → Friday closest to
  June 15; 2026 = June 1–12). Landowner elk/antelope: late **May**. Leftover sales: residents **July** (Wed closest to
  July 11), nonresidents late **July**, everyone **August** (first Wed). Bighorn regular lottery: **April–August**
  (3rd Monday in April → 1st Friday in Aug). Bighorn management lottery: 60 days before that season, 14 days.

**DRAW RULES (NE):**
> Nebraska's general bull elk draw is for residents only and uses bonus points: each year you are unsuccessful adds
> an extra entry, and Game and Parks says it is moving to a "bonus point squared" system; a resident may take only
> one antlered elk in a lifetime. Antelope and draw-unit deer use preference points, where the applicants with the
> most points are drawn first and you lose your points when you draw. Nonresidents can draw only certain deer units
> (plus statewide archery and muzzleloader) and archery antelope; most other deer units are over-the-counter "buy"
> units, and leftovers go on sale in July and August. Bighorn sheep is a resident-only random lottery with no
> points, a once-in-a-lifetime ram, and applications from April to early August. The main elk, deer and antelope
> application period is in June.

## PART 2 — HUNT UNIT BOUNDARY LAYERS

### New Mexico — NMDGF Game Management Units (owner: keary.howley@dgf.nm.gov_nmdgf, org nmdgf)

Primary layer (used by NMDGF website map, last edit 2026-09):
`https://services2.arcgis.com/CjbW1bVhK4dB3WOa/arcgis/rest/services/NMDGF_Game_Management_Units_I_E__v2_WFL1/FeatureServer/0/query`
- Unit id field: **`GMU`** (string). Format: bare number or number+uppercase letter, no prefix/padding:
  `1, 2A, 2B, 2C, 3, 4, 5A, 5B, 6A, 6B, 6C, 7…16A–16E, 17…21A, 21B…34…51A, 51B…55A, 55B…59`. 70 features.
  No unit 44 exists. No parent "16" feature — units are split into separate polygons (2A–C, 5A–B, 6A–C, 16A–E,
  21A–B, 51A–B, 55A–B).
- Label field: `GMU` (displayField). Also `GMU_Desc` (legal boundary text), `BearZone`, `Cougar_Zone`, `ACRES`, `SQMI`,
  `GMU_PDF`, `Maps`. Native SR 3857; maxRecordCount 2000.
- Layer 1 of same service = "Game_Management_Units_Subunits" (1 feature: "Portion of GMU 10").
- Alternate: `.../Game_Management_Units_All_Viewers/FeatureServer/0`, field `Game_Management_Unit`, same 70 ids
  (last edit 2025-05).

Tests (`?where=GMU='X'&outFields=*&returnGeometry=true&outSR=4326&f=geojson`):
- `GMU='34'` → 1 feature, Polygon; ACRES 1,085,487; SQMI 1696; BearZone 12; Cougar_Zone Q.
- `GMU='16B'` → 1 feature, Polygon; ACRES 603,341; SQMI 943; BearZone 10; Cougar_Zone J.
- `GMU='16'` → 0 features (must use sub-unit letters).

### Nebraska — NGPC (org services5 IOshH1zLrIieqrNk, owner NGPC.GISAdmin org)

Separate layer per species; all use field **`UnitName`** (string, no displayField set). Native SR 3857.
Base: `https://services5.arcgis.com/IOshH1zLrIieqrNk/arcgis/rest/services/`

| Species | Layer (…/FeatureServer/0/query) | Count | UnitName format |
|---|---|---|---|
| Elk | `Elk_Hunting_Units_2022` (last edit 2026-07) | 16 | `Unit 1`…`Unit 12`, `Unit 131`, `Unit 132`, `Unit 14`, `Unit 15` |
| Deer | `Deer_Mangement_Units_2022` (sic; last edit 2026-07) | 18 | Title case names: `Blue Northwest, Blue Southeast, Buffalo, Calamus East, Calamus West, Elkhorn, Frenchman, Keya Paha, Loup East, Loup West, Missouri, Pine Ridge, Plains, Platte, Republican, Sandhills, Upper Platte, Wahoo` |
| Antelope | `Antelope_Hunting_Units_2022` | 21 | Names, plus `UnitType` ("Antelope Firearm" / "Antelope Late Firearm" / "Antelope Muzzleloader") — e.g. `North Sioux` and `North Sioux Late` are separate polygons |
| Bighorn | `Bighorn_Hunting_Units_2022` | 1 | `PINE RIDGE` (all caps) |

Important: **NE elk units are now numbered** ("Unit 1"…), not named — there is no "Pine Ridge" elk unit. 166 NAC 3
also lists elk units 1–14 by district office (Unit 13 appears split into 131/132 in GIS). "Pine Ridge" exists as a
deer unit and as the single bighorn unit. Other NGPC deer layers exist (`AOSC_Deer_Units`,
`Additional_AOSC_Deer_Units`, `Additional_Antlerless_Deer_2025`, `MT_Lion_Hunting_Units_2022`) — special-permit overlays.

Tests (`?where=UnitName='X'&outFields=*&returnGeometry=true&outSR=4326&f=geojson`):
- Elk `UnitName='Unit 1'` → 1 Polygon (NW corner, starts ~-103.65, 43.00); props FID 39, Hunting_Li guides URL.
- Bighorn `UnitName='PINE RIDGE'` → 1 Polygon; props FID 1, Links guides URL.
- Deer `UnitName='Pine Ridge'` → 1 Polygon; props FID 48, UnitType null.
- Antelope `UnitName='North Sioux'` → 1 Polygon; UnitType "Antelope Firearm".
Queries are case-insensitive (deer `UnitName='PINE RIDGE'` also returns 1), but stored casing differs by layer
("PINE RIDGE" bighorn vs "Pine Ridge" deer) — normalize when displaying.
