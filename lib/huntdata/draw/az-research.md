# Arizona (AZGFD) research: draw rules + GMU boundary source

Researched 2026-10-05. Sources are AZGFD only: azgfd.com pages, AZGFD-published regulation PDFs (linked from
azgfd.com/hunting/regulations and hosted on AZGFD's S3 bucket), and AZGFD's own GIS servers. The 2026-27
Hunting Regulations booklet reprints the Commission rules (R12-4-104, -107, -114) verbatim, so the rule
citations below come from that reprint. apps.azsos.gov (the Admin Code site) blocks scripted access, so I
did not read it directly.

## DRAW RULES (prompt-ready summary)

Arizona uses a bonus point system, not a preference point system. Each bonus point gives one extra random
number in the draw, and only your lowest number counts. You also get one permanent point per species for
passing Arizona hunter education, plus a loyalty point once you apply for that species 5 years in a row.
Up to 20% of tags (per hunt for deer, elk, pronghorn, javelina, turkey, bear and sandhill crane; statewide
for bighorn sheep and bison) go first to the applicants with the most points, using their 1st and 2nd
choices. Nonresidents can get no more than 10% of the tags in a hunt (for sheep and bison: 10% statewide
or 1 tag, whichever is greater, and never more than 2 tags or 50% in one hunt). Nonresidents can also get
no more than half of their share in the bonus pass. Elk and pronghorn are in the winter draw (deadline
Feb 3, 2026). Deer, bighorn sheep, bison, fall turkey, fall javelina and sandhill crane are in the fall
draw (deadline June 2, 2026). Spring turkey, javelina, bison and bear are in the spring draw. Some archery
deer and some elk hunts are over the counter. You need an Arizona hunting license to apply, and you can
buy it with the application. To build a point without hunting, apply for that species' "bonus point only"
hunt number. Bighorn sheep and bison are once in a lifetime: one desert ram and one Rocky Mountain ram
ever, and one bison ever. There is no waiting period apart from that. Points are wiped out if you skip
applying for a species for 5 years in a row.

## Verified details and citations

### Bonus points, the bonus pass, loyalty and hunter education points
- **One extra random number per point.** Your lowest number is used, and a new number is drawn for each
  of the 3 passes. Group points are the average, rounded (0.5 rounds up).
  R12-4-107(B), R12-4-114(C)(2)(e).
  https://www.azgfd.com/hunting/hunt-draw-and-licenses/big-game-draw/bonus-point-process/
- **The draw has 3 passes:** (1) the bonus pass, (2) 1st/2nd choices, (3) 3rd/4th/5th choices.
  https://www.azgfd.com/hunting/hunt-draw-and-licenses/big-game-draw/draw-process/
- **Bonus pass: up to 20% of tags.** For bear, deer, elk, javelina, pronghorn, sandhill crane and turkey,
  it is up to 20% per hunt number. A hunt with 2-4 tags reserves 1 tag. For bighorn sheep and bison, it
  is up to 20% of all hunt numbers combined statewide. Tags go to the highest point group first, then the
  next highest, using only 1st and 2nd choices. Applicants with 0 points are not included. Tags left
  over from this pass go back into the regular pool. This is not limited to "max point holders": it works
  down through the point groups. R12-4-114(C)(2)(a)-(c), plus the draw-process page above.
- **Loyalty bonus point.** You earn it by applying for a hunt or a bonus point for a genus at least once a
  year for 5 years in a row. You keep it as long as you keep applying every year. R12-4-107(A).
- **Hunter education point.** You get one permanent point for each genus after you first pass an Arizona
  Hunter Education course (after 1/1/1980) or the Arizona Conservation Education course (after
  1/1/2021). You keep it when you draw. The course paperwork must reach AZGFD at least 30 days before the
  draw deadline. R12-4-107(J).
  The online version is "Ethically Hunting Arizona":
  https://www.azgfd.com/education/hunter-education-2/hunter-education-classes/ethically-hunting-arizona/
- **When points are used up.** Your species points go to zero when you draw a tag in the draw. They are
  also lost if you skip applying for that genus for 5 years in a row, or if you buy a surrendered tag. A
  first-come leftover tag does not use up your points. R12-4-107(F), (H).
- **Species with bonus points:** bear, bighorn sheep, bison, deer, elk, javelina, pronghorn, sandhill
  crane and turkey. R12-4-107(B).

### Nonresident caps (R12-4-114(E)-(H); E2 amendments took effect July 1, 2026)
- **Deer, elk, javelina, pronghorn, sandhill crane and turkey.** Nonresidents get no more than 10% of the
  tags in any hunt number, rounded down. For antlered deer, bull elk, pronghorn, crane or turkey hunts
  with 10 or fewer tags, nonresidents get at most 1 tag. A hunt with only 1 tag is for residents only.
- **Bighorn sheep and bison.** Nonresidents get no more than 1 tag or 10% of the total tags in a draw,
  whichever is greater. They never get more than 50% or more than 2 tags in any single hunt number.
- **Bonus pass.** Nonresidents get no more than half of their available tags in the bonus pass
  (R12-4-114(G)).
- **Other caps.** The cap also covers archery deer over-the-counter tags. Nonresidents get 10% of the
  5-year average of those tag sales (R12-4-114(H)). The Commission can raise nonresident numbers at a
  public meeting (R12-4-114(F)). The 10% cap does not apply to Arizona lifetime license holders who
  have moved out of state (regs booklet, p. 7 area).

### Which draw covers which species (2026 cycle)
- **Winter ("Midwinter") draw: elk and pronghorn.** Deadline Feb 3, 2026, 11:59 pm Arizona time. The
  booklet is "2026 Pronghorn and Elk Regulations."
  The 2027 elk and pronghorn booklet was not posted yet as of this research. It normally comes out around
  December.
  https://azgfd-live-portal-wordpress-pantheon.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/20163130/2026-Pronghorn-and-Elk-Regulations_251223.pdf
- **Fall draw: deer, fall turkey, fall javelina, bighorn sheep, bison and sandhill crane.** Deadline June
  2, 2026. First-come leftovers opened July 6 (mail) and July 13 (offices).
  https://azgfd-live-portal-wordpress-pantheon.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/20151617/2026-27-Arizona-Hunting-Regulations.pdf
- **Spring draw: spring turkey, javelina, bison, bear (some years) and raptor capture.** The 2027 Spring
  Regulations are posted.
  https://azgfd-live-portal-wordpress-pantheon.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/08084903/2027-Spring-Regulations.pdf
  Index of all booklets: https://www.azgfd.com/hunting/regulations/
- **Over-the-counter (nonpermit-tag) big game.** These hunts include archery turkey, mountain lion, fall
  bear, some archery deer and some elk.
  - **Archery deer:** the tag is valid for one calendar year. Each unit has a harvest limit, and you must
    report your harvest within 48 hours, online. Nonresident tags are capped.
  - **Elk:** there are General, Archery and HAM nonpermit-tag elk areas. Examples are the St. Johns,
    Winslow-Holbrook, Zeniff, Dry Lake, Verde Valley and Alamo Lake hunt areas, units 12A/12B/13A/13B,
    and parts of 28/31/32. The nonresident tag costs $175. See pages 17-19 of the 2026 PE regs, and
    https://www.azgfd.gov/otc
  - **Javelina:** some javelina is also sold over the counter.
- **Leftover tags.** Tags left after the draw are sold first-come, first-served. They do not use up
  bonus points. A failed first-come application does not earn a point (R12-4-107(H)-(I)).

### License, bonus-point-only and PointGuard
- **License to apply.** You must have a valid Arizona hunting license on the deadline date (or the
  extended deadline). If you don't have one, you buy it with the application. R12-4-104(K)(1). Youth age
  9 and up who apply for big game must also be licensed (R12-4-104(E)(5)(b)).
- **Bonus point only.** Each Commission Order has a "BONUS POINT ONLY" hunt number. Examples: deer 1001,
  turkey 4501, javelina 5501, bighorn 6000, bison 7501. It must be your 1st choice and your only choice.
  You may file only one application per genus per draw. It costs the application fee plus a license if
  needed. You must be 10 or older by the deadline. R12-4-107(D); regs booklet p. 22.
- **One application per genus per year.** You may file only one valid application per genus each year.
  The exceptions are genera that have more than one draw a year. R12-4-104(H).
- **PointGuard / PointGuard Plus.** These are optional add-ons, about $10 per species per application.
  If you surrender a drawn tag, the points you used are restored. Details are on p. 7 of the regs
  booklet and at www.azgfd.gov/pointguard. Rule basis: R12-4-107(G) and R12-4-118.

### Bighorn sheep and bison lifetime limits
- **Lifetime limits.** You get one desert bighorn ram in a lifetime and one Rocky Mountain bighorn ram in
  a lifetime (these are separate limits), plus one bison in a lifetime. Once you have reached a limit,
  you may not apply for that animal again (R12-4-104(J)).
- **One-horned ram.** Taking a one-horned ram still counts toward the lifetime limit.
- **No waiting period.** Arizona has no waiting period after drawing a tag. The lifetime limit applies
  only once you have harvested the animal.
- **Mandatory reporting.** Bighorn sheep and bison hunts require a harvest report.

## GMU boundary source (AZGFD official GIS)

AZGFD's own GIS portal is https://maps.azgfd.com/portal. Item "Boundaries_GameMgmtUnits" is public, and
AZGFD's per-GMU web maps (for example "GMU 6B", Data Manager: AZGFD GIS) use it.

**Primary layer (80 features, the statutory GMUs):**
```
https://maps.azgfd.com/host/rest/services/SDR_GFAW-GIS-SQL1p/Boundaries_GameMgmtUnits/MapServer/0/query
```
- **ID field:** `GMUNAME`, alias "Game Management Unit Name". It is a string (length 50) in plain unit
  format: `"1"`, `"27"`, `"5A"`, `"11M"`, `"12A"`. Letters are uppercase and there is no leading zero.
  There is no unit 14.
- **Other fields:** `GF_REGION` (string "1"-"6") and `REG_NAME` (region office, e.g. "Pinetop",
  "Flagstaff"). `SQ_MILES` and `ACRES`. `LANDOWN` lists land managers. `HUNT` lists species. The link
  fields `AGFDLink`, `USFSLink`, `BLMLink`, `USFWSLink`, `Military` and `USFSName` hold HTML anchors,
  and the AGFDLink URLs are stale. `OBJECTID`.
- **Blank units.** 4 features have `GMUNAME = ''` and REG_NAME "No AZGFD Administration". These are
  tribal and other lands, so filter them out.
- **Format and access.** The native spatial reference is NAD83 UTM 12N (EPSG:26912). `f=geojson` returns
  WGS84 lon/lat. The layer supports JSON, geoJSON and PBF, and maxRecordCount is 2000. CORS is allowed:
  the server echoes the request Origin.

**Tests (curl -G with --data-urlencode; the string IDs need single quotes):**
```
curl -G ".../Boundaries_GameMgmtUnits/MapServer/0/query" \
  --data-urlencode "where=GMUNAME='27'" --data-urlencode "outFields=*" \
  --data-urlencode "returnGeometry=true" --data-urlencode "f=geojson"
```
- **GMU 27.** HTTP 200, 141 KB. A FeatureCollection with 1 feature, a Polygon. Properties: GMUNAME
  "27", GF_REGION "1", REG_NAME "Pinetop", SQ_MILES 1352.72, ACRES 865741, LANDOWN "BLM, National
  Forest, Private, State Trust Land". HUNT is "Black Bear, Bighorn Sheep, Elk, Javelina, Mountain Lion,
  Mule Deer, Merriam's Turkey, White-tailed Deer, ...", and USFSName is "Apache-Sitgreaves National
  Forest".
- **GMU 5A.** HTTP 200, 463 KB. A FeatureCollection with 1 feature, a Polygon. Properties: GMUNAME "5A",
  GF_REGION "2", REG_NAME "Flagstaff", SQ_MILES 691.70, USFSName "Coconino National Forest".

**Alternate layer (88 features, GMUs plus split hunt units):**
```
https://maps.azgfd.com/host/rest/services/SDR_GFAW-GIS-SQL1p/Boundaries_GameMgmtUnitsWithHuntUnits/FeatureServer/0/query
```
- It uses the same `GMUNAME` schema, but some GMUs are split into hunt units: 12AE/12AW, 15BE/15BW,
  22N/22S, 23N/23S, 5BN, and 7E/7W. Units 22 and 7 also still appear whole.
- Non-AZGFD land is coded `"N/A"` here instead of `''`.
- This layer suits per-hunt shading where the regs use "North/South" or "East/West" splits.

**Hunt-specific areas (hunt boundaries do differ from GMUs).** Many hunts use areas that are not whole
GMUs. The legal text for these is the "Open Areas" column plus the numbered Notes in each Commission
Order, inside the regs booklets. Examples:
- "Kanab Creek Hunt Area in Units 12A, 12B and 13A"
- "44B South (south of I-10 and that portion of Units 45A and 45B north of ...)"
- "Black River Hunt Area in Units 1 and 27"
- "4B North"
- the over-the-counter elk areas (St. Johns, Verde Valley, and others)

The GMU boundaries themselves are legally defined in R12-4-108 (Management Unit Boundaries).

AZGFD publishes polygons for these hunt areas at:
```
https://maps.azgfd.com/server/rest/services/GMUMaps/HuntAreas/FeatureServer   (also /MapServer)
  0 Elk_Sub_Units   1 Bighorn_Sheep_Sub_Units   2 Black_Bear_Sub_Units   3 Turkey_Sub_Units
```
- **Name field.** The area name is in the oddly named field
  `"AGFD_PHX\AGFDGISSDROWNER".Elk_Sub_Units.area`, alias "Area Name" (for example "St. Johns Hunt
  Area" or "4B North").
- **Other fields.** `type` / `HuntType` holds values like "OTC" and "LO". There are also `region` and
  `Active`.
- **Querying.** Query with `where=1=1`, then match on the name in the client, because the field name is
  awkward to quote.
